Jeppson Dental, a family dental practice, needed a packet a new patient could take home. The folder opens onto a printed bio of the dentist and a short list of how the practice handles a visit. A full-color insert lies across it, built around a photograph of a family and one line about the experience. The logo is on the insert and on the folder, so the packet still names Jeppson Dental after the insert comes out. That is what a [presentation folder](/products/presentation-folders/) is for.
