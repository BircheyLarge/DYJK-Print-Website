The Bagel Shop needed a menu a guest would recognize at the table. The piece is a tall card. The cover is chalkboard-style: a round logo, the line "More than just a bagel," and the word Menu set large. That is the side a guest sees before any dish. The [menu](/products/menus/) exists so the guest can choose, and the cover says The Bagel Shop before it is opened.
