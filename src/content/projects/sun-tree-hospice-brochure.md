Sun Tree Hospice needed a piece a family could open later and still follow. The brochure is a tri-fold in green and gold. Open, it explains what hospice is, introduces the medical director, and lists the services, with a family photograph across the panels. The cover carries the tree logo, a line about care for patients and their families, and the website. A [brochure](/products/brochures/) gives that explanation room in a piece someone can hold.
