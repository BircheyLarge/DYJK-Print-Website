The Lift House, a ski shop, needed cards for people asking about demos and rentals. The set has two designs. Black cards carry a white chairlift mark and The Lift House. The others use a mountain photograph, a Sons of Powder mark, and a line offering 20% off demos and rentals. One card in that row also prints a person's name. The same small [business card](/products/business-cards/) format introduces someone or states the offer.
