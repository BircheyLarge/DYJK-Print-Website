Clean It Up, a carpet-cleaning company, needed an offer that stays on the knob until someone takes it inside. The hanger is tall, with a hole at the top that fits a doorknob, and a soap-bubble design behind the name. The middle is set in plain type: the quote is what you pay, with no surprises and no hidden fees. A coupon panel at the bottom offers 20% off. A [door hanger](/products/door-hangers/) is built for that handoff.
