DeSpain The Subaru Specialist, a repair shop, needed a card that still said the specialty after it left the counter. The front is black, with a blue wordmark, a silver star, and that line under the name. The back, visible on the stack, is an engine illustration. One side names the shop. The other shows the work. Someone who finds the [business card](/products/business-cards/) later can tell the specialty from the wordmark and the engine.
